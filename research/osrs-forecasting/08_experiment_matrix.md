# Proposed preregistration — not executed

This matrix becomes effective only after review, dated registration and canonical-data checks. Existing development results cannot be relabelled confirmation. The primary unit is a forecast origin with its entire item cross-section, not millions of independent item-time samples.

## Common frozen design

Universe: up to 100 main-economy items, selected using development-only observed volume and at least 95% usable hourly coverage. Include liquid, medium and low-volume strata where feasible; freeze item IDs and a deterministic eligibility rule based on past data. Include an expanding-universe analysis for newcomers; do not claim full-market generality. Canonicalization must define source priority, duplicates, numeric regimes and bucket-end availability. Aggregate 5-minute data to hourly using side-volume weights and disjoint volumes only. Compare native hourly aggregates on overlap. Exclude guide prices, seasonal or unclassified economies, and private data from the primary track.

Use 168 hours of context and hourly increments over H = 24, with H = 4 secondary; a daily H = 7 study comes later. The primary target is cumulative log-return marginals derived from increment paths. Average CRPS over prespecified horizons of 1, 4 and 24 hours and across items, equally weighted after training-only scaling. Also publish GP-domain errors. Train on up to 365 days per rolling origin. Use three chronological 90-day validation blocks from eligible development periods strictly before reserved timestamp `1785024300`. Fix endpoints and IDs in a signed manifest before fitting. Training labels must mature before validation begins; validation features may use legitimately available earlier observations. Purge 24-hour label overlap at boundaries, extending to seven days for the later seven-day target. Do not impose an arbitrary embargo because context spans 168 hours. Preserve the prior sealed holdout untouched.

Locked final: propose a **new 56-day prospective period**, starting at the first UTC midnight at least seven days after the approved manifest is committed. Timestamp forecasts before their targets. Evaluate once after final label maturity, with an additional 24-hour collection tail for the final H = 24 origin. No final-result-based model or policy selection. Report pipeline failures and missingness; changes require a new version and future test. Never move boundaries to find a gain. Fifty-six days provides about eight nonoverlapping weekly blocks: a separately registered replication period may be necessary for precision or regime stability.

Use seeds 11, 23 and 47 for stochastic contenders. Share data and encoders, with comparable parameter counts where feasible. Early stopping uses past validation CRPS only, with at most 50 epochs. Use one published small configuration per neural contender and at most two predeclared numerical-rescue settings, subject to the total trial/fit cap. Report all attempts. Non-neural parameters receive the same validation opportunities. Likelihood comparisons require identical targets, measures, Jacobians and masks; otherwise keep them descriptive within family.

## Inference and decision rules

Define relative gain as `Δ = 1 − mean(score_candidate) / mean(score_baseline)` on paired identical eligible targets. Bootstrap **whole cross-sectional date blocks** using seven-day moving blocks, with three-day and 14-day sensitivity. Preserve overlap; additionally evaluate origins spaced 24 hours apart. Use 10,000 final bootstrap replicates if runtime permits, recording Monte Carlo error. Item strata are descriptive, not independent replication.

Apply Holm correction at familywise level 0.05 to C1, C2 and C3. Report paired 95% intervals descriptively and explicit Holm-adjusted tests for the claims. If adjusted intervals are reported, their construction must match the familywise claims. E5–E8 remain exploratory or separately registered. Failure of an improvement gate is no-go for that complexity claim, not proof of market efficiency.

Calibration point thresholds do not replace uncertainty. If the coverage interval cannot resolve the 87–93% tolerance, or the improvement interval includes zero, label the result **inconclusive**. Tail Brier scores need prespecified exceedances and sufficient positives and negatives; fewer than 50 positive events is descriptive only. The event threshold is a heuristic, not a power theorem. Development variance and block sensitivity determine whether 56 days needs separately registered replication; do not continue optionally until significance.

Check joint sample covariance/autocorrelation and grid-observable first-passage, maximum and drawdown scores. Preserve marginals in shuffled-dependence ablations. ProFITi-style queries must not silently change the requested time set. Any proposed pre/post-September-30 analysis is limited to permitted development data or separately registered prospective observations: it does not authorize reading sealed original-export holdout rows. Existing aggregate source-quality summaries may inform the design without becoming an untouched efficacy test.

## Budget reconciliation

Primary design: **six neural configurations × three seeds = 18 seeded trial pipelines**. Each pipeline requires three validation-fold fits plus one final refit: **72 fits total**. At an assumed 0.5–4 hours per fit, the planning range is **36–288 GPU-hours**. Configurations: diagonal Gaussian, low-rank Gaussian, matched Student-t, spline flow, shared-encoder independent-horizon control and recursive control. Reuse E2–E4 trial pipelines; table entries do not authorize extra fits.

This is a conditional ceiling, not authorization to execute every fit. Begin with **three individual fits as smoke/pilot checks**, then review measured memory, throughput and projected campaign cost before continuation. Stop weaker contenders on validation according to the frozen selection rule; do not use the final period for that decision. Every numerical rescue consumes a fit slot and GPU time; any replacement/reduction must be explicit in the manifest. Independent hardware replication requires substitution within the same limits or separate approval. Fix exact per-fit allocations before approval. Conditional flow matching, GP priors and E5–E8 ablations remain deferred.
