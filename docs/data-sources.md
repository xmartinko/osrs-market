**Structural break: 30 September 2026.** As stated by the [Wiki price documentation](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/RuneScape:Real-time_Prices), earlier real-time data came from RuneLite; subsequent data comes from Jagex and covers all game clients. Pre/post volumes have different coverage semantics. Canonical Wiki rows retain a `runelite_only` / `jagex_all_clients` tag. Historical mirrors also straddle this break. Fractional v2 prices are stored as float64.

“Adopt” means a working private acquisition adapter and an actual successful response, not approval to redistribute or a production dependency. “Optional” includes acquired material with weaker provenance or redundant coverage. ## What the archives actually contain

The [seasonal repository README](https://github.com/weirdgloop/runelite-ge-dumps) says it hosts concluded seasonal economies. The actual five archives contain four Deadman economies **and Fresh Start Worlds**, not main-economy history. Their canonical partitions retain `economy=deadman_reborn`, `fsw_2022`, `deadman_apocalypse`, `deadman_armageddon`, or `deadman_annihilation`. They are never merged with `main`.

The 5m Kaggle mirror contains 4,539 IDs. Twenty-six are not in today's 4,674-ID mapping. They cannot safely be called current main-economy items just from their numeric IDs. Both the raw files and earlier conversions remain intact; `canonical-v2/main` and `canonical-v2/unclassified_legacy` are the qualified conversions used in coverage totals.

An additional local adapter, `wiki-feature-facts`, extracts literal parameters from `Infobox Item`, `Infobox Bonuses`, `DropsLine` and `StoreLine` templates in the archived pages. Its initial conversion produced **181,600** facts. For example, the whip page has `weight=0.453` and `aslash=+82`; numeric literals become floats, while nested templates/links remain strings with a null numeric value. Revision IDs, revision timestamps, template offsets and upstream hashes preserve attribution and identity. This is conservative extraction of definitions, not template execution or a claim of complete realized supply. Current Wiki text complements the older osrsbox snapshots.

Module revisions contain fields such as `itemId`, `price`, `date`, sometimes `volume` and `volumeDate`. Recent modules may contain references to other Lua data rather than literals. The adapter preserves text and parses only numeric literals; it never executes Lua, substitutes zero for `nil`, or equates revision time with price time. The 83 sampled revisions include **75 literal prices**, but repeated values across edits are not new trades. These pages share Wiki/Weird Gloop provenance; independence has **not** been established. `Module:Exchange/Abyssal whip/Data` and `Module:GEMW/data` were missing, not hidden daily datasets.

Wayback's 2019 whip chart includes 1,521 distinct source tokens at 1,502 timestamps. Conflicting timestamps are tagged and retained, not averaged or silently deduplicated. Only 290 tokens include a usable volume field, first dated **2018-09-25**. Capture time is separate from price time. The 2013 page proves a page existed, not that a daily market history exists. OSRS's GE itself opened **26 February 2015**, as recorded on the [GE page](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Grand_Exchange); earlier player-to-player guide prices would be a different series.

## Volume availability

The earliest non-null Weird Gloop `all` volume is **2018-02-19**, across a limited set of items. This is not a universal start date. For IDs **2** and **4151**, first volume is **2018-09-25**; for **561**, **2018-02-19**. Common item-level first-volume dates also include 2018-12-08, 2018-12-10 and 2018-12-25. Nulls remain in later years. Exact annual coverage and per-item first/last null dates are in the coverage artifact and [report](data-coverage.md).

Wiki bulk/mirror volume is present from the measured 2021-03-08 start, including legitimate zeros. Rolling Weird Gloop has **1,358,139 non-null-volume rows out of 1,358,437**: all **298 Old school bond (13190) rows lack volume**. Its other collected items have volume throughout their observed window; 97 requested IDs returned no results at all. Latest-trade snapshots, official guide graphs, the academic CSV and the bond mirror have **no volume**. Metadata drop rates describe possible drops; they do not measure kills, realized supply or item removals. No complete item-sink count series was obtained.

## Policy, rate limits and evidence

All requests in the finishing execution use exactly `osrs-market-research/0.1 (personal research; contact noreply@example.invalid)`. Client policy is one active request per host, at least **1.05 seconds between starts**, shared across acquisition processes by host locks. Different hosts may proceed simultaneously. Retries are limited to three after the first attempt, for transport failures, HTTP 429 and 5xx; exponential backoff adds jitter, and `Retry-After` seconds/HTTP dates take precedence. Authentication denials and anti-bot pages are terminal. Redirects are followed only as ordinary public download redirects, carrying the same UA. No credentials, cookies, login sessions, proxies or challenge solving are used.

No provider-enforced numerical limit was established for Wiki, Weird Gloop, official graphs or world lists; the successful acquisition rate is **not a licence to increase it**. GitHub's response headers advertise its unauthenticated quota; exact observed limit and remaining values are in the HTTP audit. Do not deliberately exhaust quotas to discover a maximum. [Wiki guidance](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/RuneScape:Real-time_Prices) requests an identifiable UA. [Weird Gloop licensing](https://meta.weirdgloop.org/w/Licensing) and [Jagex terms](https://legal.jagex.com/docs/terms) remain applicable; “public endpoint” does not mean unrestricted redistribution.

Four earlier probe artifacts already on disk use an alternative descriptive UA (`private personal research; no contact address`). This is a recorded provenance limitation, not silently relabelled as compliant. The current client uses the exact requested UA. The audit records all observed variants and can fail on any nonconforming request after an explicit run start. Earlier evidence without request-start timestamps cannot prove start spacing retrospectively.

Exact HTTP evidence is retained as `evidence/http/<sha256(url)>-<request-nanoseconds>.json` with a sibling `.body`: URL, status, headers, fetched-at, UA, transport error and payload hash. An HTTP status of `0` means **no HTTP response**, not an invented server status. HTTP 200 with an empty body or application-level error is not counted as a working data source. Mutable URL indexes and checkpoints reference immutable response payloads. See the coverage report for the actual audit output, including any 429/5xx encountered.
